The application includes remote rooms, same-device play, Classic, Endgames, 3–4 Players, and Chaos Kingdom (formerly Custom Arena). Read [docs/MULTIPLAYER_RULES.md](docs/MULTIPLAYER_RULES.md) for the three- and four-player variant. Chaos Kingdom has 8 × 4 / 4 × 8 / 8 × 8 boards and editable starting layouts. Teammates cannot capture/check one another or control each other's pieces; occupied ally squares block movement. Only the moving player's king must be safe. Team results retain both winning colors (`winners`, `winningTeam`) and the affected color (`defeated`), with a representative `winner` only for compatibility. Preserve the result through local/remote snapshots, replay, reconnect and persistence, and show both winners in the UI.
